A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Missouri–Columbia, specializing in wide-bandgap and ultra-wide-bandgap semiconductor devices, power electronics, and device reliability.

My research focuses on GaN HEMTs and β-Ga2O3 MOSFETs, investigating radiation-induced transient effects, high-temperature behavior, carrier transport, and device performance using TCAD-based computational modeling.

I also integrate machine learning with semiconductor device physics to accelerate parameter extraction, inverse modeling, and design optimization. My experience extends to semiconductor characterization, fabrication, and university-level teaching and mentorship.

MY RESEARCH VISION

Reliable Electronics for Extreme Environments.
