About Me

Exploring the Future of Semiconductor Technology

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Missouri–Columbia, working under the supervision of Prof. Syed Kamrul Islam.

My research focuses on wide- and ultra-wide-bandgap semiconductor devices, device reliability under extreme operating environments, and the integration of machine learning with physics-based device modeling.

I am particularly interested in bridging semiconductor device physics, computational simulations, and data-driven optimization to advance next-generation power electronics.
